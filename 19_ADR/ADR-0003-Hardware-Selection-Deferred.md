# ADR-0003 - Hardware Selection Deferred Until Requirements

Status: ACCEPTED_FOR_BASELINE

Decision:
No MCU/MPU/FPGA is selected in RUN-000.

Required inputs to the future trade study:
- I/O count/types
- latency/jitter
- control-loop rates
- memory
- network/protocols
- isolation
- safety architecture
- secure boot/update
- diagnostics
- environmental constraints
- lifecycle/supply chain
- toolchain/reproducibility
- certification implications

Candidate families may be researched but not promoted before GATE-005.
