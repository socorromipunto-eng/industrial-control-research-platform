# ADR-0004 - Safety Claim Boundary

Status: ACCEPTED_FOR_BASELINE

Decision:
ICRP may research interlocks, E-stop/ESD logic, safe states and functional
safety methods, but no SIL/PL/certified-safety claim is made without exact
requirements, architecture, component capability, validation and applicable
third-party assessment.

Consequence:
Simulation and software test evidence remain correctly scoped.
