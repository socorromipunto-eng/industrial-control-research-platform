# ADR-0001 - Independent Industrial-Control Research Boundary

Status: ACCEPTED_FOR_BASELINE

Decision:
ICRP is a separate research project. Guardian, EUDI/TrustMesh and third-party
client repositories may provide methodological lessons only. Their product code,
confidential material and domain claims are not imported by implication.

Consequence:
All ICRP requirements, architecture, evidence and claims must stand on ICRP
sources and artifacts.
