# ICRP Source of Truth

Precedence for project decisions:
1. Applicable law/regulation and authoritative legal source.
2. Applicable current normative standards/profiles.
3. Approved project constitution/policies.
4. Approved requirements.
5. Approved architecture and ADRs.
6. Approved PCNs/hazard analyses.
7. Approved implementation/configuration.
8. Reproducible validation evidence.
9. Approved as-built/handover.
10. Memory/conversation only as discovery context.

Conflicts are resolved upward, never silently downward.
