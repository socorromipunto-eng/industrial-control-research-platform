# Normative-First Rule

Before any design, code, hardware selection, safety claim, cybersecurity claim,
test-plan promotion, or compliance statement:

1. Identify the exact normative object being evaluated.
2. Identify jurisdiction and deployment context.
3. Identify governing law/regulation where applicable.
4. Identify applicable standard/profile/guidance.
5. Verify current version, status, effective date and supersession.
6. Record official source and provenance.
7. Separate applicability from satisfaction.
8. Separate current evidence state from gap/trigger state.
9. Derive action only from documented requirements and evidence.
10. FAIL CLOSED when applicability, version, evidence or authority is unresolved.

Every gate must answer:
"What normative uncertainty does this gate eliminate?"
