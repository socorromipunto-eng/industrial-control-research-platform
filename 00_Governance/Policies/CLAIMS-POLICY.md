# Claims Policy

Allowed status vocabulary:
DISCOVERED, SUPPORTED, TESTED, CONFORMANT, VALIDATED, AUTHORIZED, ACCEPTED.

Rules:
- Do not skip statuses by implication.
- A runner PASS proves only its explicitly defined gate.
- No repository artifact may self-award regulatory conformity.
- Third-party certification/validation scope must match the exact component,
  version, configuration, build and operating boundary.
- Draft standards and draft government publications are informative until
  promoted by an explicit governed decision.
