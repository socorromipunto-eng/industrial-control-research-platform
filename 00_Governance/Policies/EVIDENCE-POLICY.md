# Evidence Policy

Material actions shall capture, where applicable:

ACTION_ID
REQUEST_ID
ACTOR_ID
COMPONENT_ID
BUILD_ID
POLICY_ID
POLICY_HASH
NORMATIVE_SOURCE_ID
PRE_STATE_HASH
DECISION
AUTHORIZATION
EXECUTION_RESULT
POST_STATE_HASH
VALIDATION_RESULT
EVIDENCE_HASH
TIMESTAMP_UTC
SIGNATURE_OR_ATTESTATION

Evidence classes:
E0 assertion only
E1 documentation
E2 configuration/state capture
E3 reproducible test result
E4 independently reproduced result
E5 signed/attested authoritative evidence
