# Change Management Policy

Every material change requires:
1. Change Request ID.
2. Reason and trigger.
3. Affected requirements/architecture/components.
4. Safety impact.
5. Cybersecurity impact.
6. Normative impact.
7. Evidence impact.
8. Regression scope.
9. Rollback/recovery plan.
10. Human authorization before mutation.
11. Post-change validation.
12. As-built update.

No hidden mutations.
